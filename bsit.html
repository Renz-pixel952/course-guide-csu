BS Information Technology

Build the digital future. BSIT prepares you to design, develop, and manage technology systems that power businesses and communities across the Philippines and beyond.

4 Years Board Exam Available High Demand Remote Work Friendly

📖 What You'll Learn

  • Programming (C, C++, Java, Python)
  • Web and Mobile Development
  • Database Management Systems
  • Networking and Cybersecurity
  • Systems Analysis and Design
  • Artificial Intelligence basics

💼 Career Paths

  • Web / Mobile Developer
  • Software Engineer
  • Cybersecurity Analyst
  • Database Administrator
  • IT Support Specialist
  • Freelancer / Remote Worker

🌏 Where You Can Work

  • Tech companies local and international
  • Banks and financial institutions
  • Government agencies
  • Schools and universities
  • Hospitals and healthcare systems
  • Work from home remote jobs

📋 Subjects Overview

  • Computer Programming 1 and 2
  • Data Structures and Algorithms
  • Web Development
  • Operating Systems
  • Software Engineering
  • Capstone Project

💰 Average Starting Salary in Philippines

₱20,000 – ₱35,000
per month as a fresh graduate · Senior IT professionals can earn ₱80,000 or more

✅ Is BSIT Right for You?

  • You enjoy using computers and technology
  • You like solving problems logically
  • You are curious about how apps and websites work
  • You want a career with many job opportunities
  • You are open to continuous learning
  • You want the option to work from home or abroad
