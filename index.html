Student Advice

6 Tips Before Choosing

Choosing a course is a big decision. Here are some simple things to consider.

01

❤️ Know Your Interests

Think about the subjects and activities that genuinely make you excited to learn.

02

💪 Know Your Strengths

Consider what you're naturally good at and what skills you want to develop.

03

💼 Research Careers

Look at possible jobs and career paths connected to each course.

04

🗣️ Ask Other People

Talk to students, teachers, graduates, and professionals about their experiences.

05

🔄 Don't Be Afraid

Your first decision doesn't necessarily determine your entire future.

06

🌟 Trust Yourself

After doing your research, believe in yourself and take the next step.
