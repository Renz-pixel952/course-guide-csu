Before You Decide

Things to Consider

Choosing a course is an important decision. Think about these factors before making your choice.

01

Know Your Interests

Think about the subjects, activities, and topics that naturally interest you.

02

Consider Your Strengths

Identify the skills you already have and the areas where you enjoy learning.

03

Think About Your Career

Consider what type of career or profession you would like to pursue in the future.

04

Research the Program

Learn about the subjects, requirements, activities, and possible careers of the course.

05

Ask for Advice

Talk with teachers, family members, students, and professionals when possible.

06

Choose With Confidence

Take time to think about your decision and choose a path that fits your goals.
