Before Choosing Your Course

Consider these important factors before deciding on your college program.

01

Know Your Interests

Think about the subjects and activities that you genuinely enjoy.

02

Identify Your Strengths

Consider the skills and subjects where you perform well.

03

Think About Your Career

Consider the profession or career you would like to pursue.

04

Research the Program

05

06

Take your time and choose a program that fits your goals and interests.
