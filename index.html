Helpful Advice

Tips for Choosing the Right Course

01

Know Your Interests

What subjects do you enjoy most in school? Your interests are a strong indicator of what course you'll love.

02

Think About Your Strengths

Are you good with numbers? Creative? A natural leader? Choose a course that plays to your strengths.

03

Consider Job Opportunities

Research what careers are in demand. Think about where you want to be 5 to 10 years from now.

04

Talk to People

Ask students, teachers, and professionals about their experience. Real stories help you decide better.

05

It's Okay to Change

Many successful people shifted courses. What matters is you keep moving forward and never stop learning.

06

Trust Your Gut

After all the research, trust yourself. You know your dreams better than anyone else does.
