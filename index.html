ABOUT THE CAMPUS

Cagayan State University — Sanchez Mira Campus

Cagayan State University — Sanchez Mira Campus provides higher education opportunities through various undergraduate degree programs.

The campus offers programs in agriculture, business and accounting, engineering, hospitality, industrial technology, information technology, education, and criminology.
