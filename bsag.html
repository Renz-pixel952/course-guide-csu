BS Agriculture

Feed the nation and sustain the environment. BSAg teaches you modern farming, crop science, and agricultural technology to help communities grow and thrive.

4 Years Board Exam Available Government Jobs High Demand in Rural Areas

📖 What You'll Learn

  • Crop science and production
  • Animal science and husbandry
  • Soil science and management
  • Agricultural technology
  • Farm management and business
  • Sustainable and organic farming

💼 Career Paths

  • Agricultural Technician
  • Farm Manager
  • Agricultural Extension Worker
  • DA / BFAR Government Employee
  • Agri-Entrepreneur
  • Agricultural Researcher

🌏 Where You Can Work

  • Department of Agriculture (DA)
  • Bureau of Fisheries (BFAR)
  • Local Government Units (LGU)
  • Agricultural cooperatives
  • Private farms and plantations
  • Research institutions

📋 Subjects Overview

  • General Agriculture
  • Crop Science
  • Animal Production
  • Soil and Water Management
  • Agricultural Economics
  • Farm Practicum / OJT

💰 Average Starting Salary in Philippines

₱18,000 – ₱28,000
per month starting salary · Government agriculture employees receive additional benefits and allowances

✅ Is BSAg Right for You?

  • You love nature, farming, and the outdoors
  • You want to help rural communities grow
  • You are hardworking and not afraid to get your hands dirty
  • You are interested in plants, animals, and soil
  • You want a career that feeds and sustains communities
  • You dream of running your own farm or agri-business
